## Explicitly outside the initial scope

Online payment processing, card storage, complex chat, external push/email notifications beyond account email flows, and multi-tenant SaaS are not required. No gateway or external notification delivery is claimed. Per-event coach overrides and expiry dates are optional enhancements; archival status already satisfies the announcement archive/expiry requirement.
